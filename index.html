Stardust Partners

Stardust Partners is a Los Angeles real estate firm for high-end adaptive reuse. We buy underloved buildings and bring them back through thoughtful design, curated tenancy and impeccable execution. Projects include Signal, Astral and 50 Varick.

Paolo Carini and Raan Parton founded Stardust Partners in 2016 to bring a sophisticated, design-driven product to a commoditized market. With a precise vision aligning product and tenant, the firm creates value by repositioning buildings others pass over.

In 2025 Lindsay Parton joined as Executive Chairman. Lindsay is a California developer with more than 40 years of experience and a portfolio of developed and managed properties of over $3 billion.

Approach

Deal sourcing

Sector expertise, deep relationships and a sharp eye for underloved buildings let us acquire below market and capture the upside through design.

Acquisition

We focus on distressed existing properties in urban neighborhoods, where thoughtful redevelopment and active repositioning unlock opportunistic returns.

Development

One development and asset management approach across all our properties: cohesive, design-led execution, quality and refinement without overspending.

Tenancy

Direct access to a wide range of tenants allows a targeted co-tenancy plan. Leasing begins before the property is acquired.

The second project with Klein Agency after Signal: offices, food and drink and specialty retail under one roof, with a pavilion and terrace on the roof.
